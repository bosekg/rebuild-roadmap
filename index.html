Pillar 1

Skool community

A community that gives real value from the day someone joins. Free to get in, paid to go deeper.

Free

The Rebuild Community

Free modules carry the same amount of value as William's YouTube videos, or a bit more. Someone who never pays should still leave with something real.

Paid

The Rebuild Circle

Paid modules are built in levels. Members start with a foundation, then go deeper into William's own story.

Level 1

Foundation

The base everyone goes through first, so every member is working from the same understanding.

Level 2

William's story

Once the foundation is in place, William walks them through his own journey and what it taught him.

Classroom structure

Three sections, with the mental and physical tracks kept strictly separate.

  • Start Here: orientation for everyone entering
  • Mental & Mind Improvement: the mental guide
  • Physical Elevation: training structure, blueprints, nutrition

SOPs and interaction for new members

Whoever enters the community should get real value straight away, not an empty room. We build SOPs for how new members are welcomed and moved through, plus interactive pieces that give them something to do and people to talk to.

Flag · launch seeding

Before the public launch, share the community with friends so they join first. A community that already has people in it is easier to join than an empty one.

Pillar 2

Content engine

YouTube long-form is the pillar. Short-form is extracted from it. Both offers get marketed, each with its own angle.

Long-form

YouTube

About 10 minute A-to-B value videos, each attacking one ICP pain point. Filmed in batches, so there is always a buffer of finished videos.

Short-form

Extracted from the videos

The strongest moments of each long-form video become standalone shorts. One shoot feeds both formats.

Two offers, two different pain points

Community

"Be around people like you"

Start by establishing that the environment and the people around you matter. That attacks the pain point of being surrounded by the wrong environment. Then the offer: if you want to be among like-minded people just like yourself, join the Rebuild community.

1:1 mentorship

Personal guidance

Built on the ICP pain points that need someone in your corner, such as inconsistency, knowing what to do but not doing it, and feeling stuck. These point to the DM keyword and the sales process.

Everyday engagement

Normal daily engagement on the socials, kept inside the frame of William's brand: how he talks, what he stands for, and who he's talking to.

Pillar 3

Sales process

The goal is a William who is good at sales calls, and a funnel that keeps getting tighter.

Practice

Roleplays

Separate practice calls over 2-3 weeks to get through every scenario and become familiar with the script. Then constant improvement.

Optimize

Funnel tweaks

As real conversations come in, the funnel gets adjusted wherever it leaks.

Future

App setter & closer

The long-term plan is to delegate this part to a setter and a closer. William has to learn the process himself first.
